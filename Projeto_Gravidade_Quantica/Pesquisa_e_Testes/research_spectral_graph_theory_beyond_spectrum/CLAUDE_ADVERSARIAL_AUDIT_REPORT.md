**Theorem 4.2 (Cartan $A_{m-1}$ dispersion).** I recomputed the Dirichlet moments from scratch: $\mathrm{Var}(x_i)=(m-1)/(m^2(m\alpha+1))$, $\mathrm{Cov}(x_i,x_j)=-1/(m^2(m\alpha+1))$, and propagated these into $\mathrm{Var}(z_i)=2/(m(m\alpha+1))$, $\mathrm{Cov}(z_i,z_{i+1})=-1/(m(m\alpha+1))$, $\mathrm{Cov}(z_i,z_j)=0$ for $|i-j|\ge2$. Moderate, non-fatal.
