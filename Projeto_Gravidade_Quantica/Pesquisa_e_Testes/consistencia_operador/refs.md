## Trechos literais usados (texto completo)

- **Pospelov–Shang 2012, conclusões:** "Given that various phenomenological constraints on dimension 4 LV operators are more stringent than 10⁻²⁰, one would need to have Λ_HL ≲ 10¹⁰ GeV."
- **BPS 2011, eqs. (5.16)–(5.17):** "M∗ ≲ M_α … The experimental bound (5.35) … constrains M_α to be lower than 10¹⁵ ÷ 10¹⁶ GeV. According to (5.16) this translates into the bound M∗ ≲ 10¹⁵ ÷ 10¹⁶ GeV."
- **BPS 2011, eq. (5.21):** time-lag data from AGN and GRBs give "M∗(mat) ≳ 10¹⁰ ÷ 10¹¹ GeV", and "this lower bound must be taken cautiously: its validity depends on the details of the matter sector."
- **BPS 2011, resumo:** "the only model which is free from instabilities and strong coupling is the non-projectable one."
- **GSS 2018:** "having M∗ < M_SC … can resolve the strong coupling problem"; "The strong coupling scale M_SC is bounded from below by the Lorentz breaking scale M⋆. M⋆ is in turn bounded from below by observations …"
- **CES 2013, resumo:** "a crucial property, namely positivity of their solutions, is not preserved automatically"; Sec. II: "the resulting probability densities for the probe particle are no longer positive semidefinite. Thus, strictly speaking, the 'quantum-improved' diffusion equations do not give rise to a well-defined diffusion process." The paper treats the Hořava–Lifshitz case explicitly (its eq. 7).
- **Anselmi–Piva 2018, resumo:** "the width of χ_μν, which is negative, shows that the theory predicts the violation of causality at energies larger than the fakeon mass."
