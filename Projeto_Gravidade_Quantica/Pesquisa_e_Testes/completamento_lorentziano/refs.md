## Trechos literais usados

- **ABP 2020, resumo:** "The consistency of the approach puts a lower bound (m_χ > m_φ/4) on the mass m_χ of the
  fakeon with respect to the mass m_φ of the inflaton. The tensor-to-scalar ratio r is predicted within less than an
  order of magnitude (4/3 < N²r < 12 to the leading order in the number of e-foldings N)."
- **ABP 2020, eq. 7.3 e §7:** r = 24m_χ²/[N²(m_φ²+2m_χ²)]; "the predictions for A_T, r and n_T are smaller by a
  factor 2m_χ²/(m_φ²+2m_χ²)"; "for N = 60 we have 0.4 ≲ 1000r ≲ 3".
- **BBMM 2016, resumo:** "We find a universal dimensional reduction to 2 dimensions, in all dimensions."
- **BBMM 2016, §III:** "the universal dimensional reduction to d_s = 2, crucially relies on the regularisation of the
  Green functions"; "this propagator is not a Green function of the original retarded d'Alembertians".
- **BBMM 2016, §IV:** "whether the improved UV behaviour of the propagator … [is] inextricably linked to negative norm
  states"; Aslanbeigi–Saravani com ρ ≥ 0: "the spectral dimension does not reduce to 2".
- **ASS 2014, resumo:** "for large p it becomes constant"; "evidence that the original 4D causal set d'Alembertian
  is unstable".
- **Anselmi–Piva PRD 2017, resumo:** "formulated as nonanalytically Wick rotated Euclidean theories"; "the same
  higher-derivative models violate unitarity if they are formulated directly in Minkowski spacetime".
- **Kubo–Kugo 2023, resumo:** "complex ghosts are actually created and unitarity is violated … The theories are
  unitary and renormalizable below the threshold."
- **Platania–Wetterich 2020, resumo:** "These 'fake ghosts' do not indicate a violation of unitarity."
- **Bonanno et al. 2022, resumo:** "the spectral function of the latter [background] necessarily has negative parts
  … the spectral function of the dynamical graviton is positive."
- **Fehre et al. 2023, resumo:** "We find a positive graviton spectral function".
- **Smilga 2009, resumo:** "Generically, the inclusion of interaction terms breaks unitarity"; na realização de
  Bender–Mannheim, "wave functions grow exponentially".
- **LSS 2013, resumo:** "β ≳ 10⁻⁶ m⁻¹" (Gravity Probe B) e "β ≳ 10⁴ m⁻¹" (balanças de torção).
- **ACT DR6 (Louis et al.), resumo:** "a spectral index of n_s = 0.974 ± 0.003".
- **Lee et al. 2020, resumo:** "limiting with 95% confidence any gravitational-strength Yukawa interactions to ranges
  < 38.6 μm".
- **Lauscher–Reuter 2005, resumo:** "On sub-Planckian distances it predicts that spacetime is a fractal with an
  effective dimensionality of 2."
