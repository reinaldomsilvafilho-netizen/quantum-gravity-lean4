## 1. The 12 Epistemic Criteria for a Theory of Everything

To evaluate candidate theories of fundamental physics without bias, we define **12 objective scientific criteria** spanning mathematical rigor, empirical explanatory power, and falsifiability:

1. **Ultraviolet Finiteness (UV-Reg)**: Complete absence of unphysical infinities in self-energies, loop integrals, and scattering amplitudes.
2. **Physical Spacetime Dimension (4D-Dim)**: Operation in macroscopic 4D spacetime without postulating unobserved compactified spatial dimensions.
3. **Parameter Predictability (Param-Pred)**: Derivation or severe reduction of the 26+ arbitrary parameters of the Standard Model.
4. **Singularity Resolution (Sing-Res)**: Elimination of curvature blow-up ($R \to \infty$) at the Big Bang and in black hole cores via non-singular bounces.
5. **Information Unitarity & Measurement (Unit-Meas)**: Preservation of quantum unitarity (Page curve) coupled to a dynamical, non-paradoxical objective collapse mechanism.
6. **Background Independence (Bg-Indep)**: Formulation where geometry is dynamical and non-perturbative, rather than expanding around a fixed background.
7. **Gauge & Chiral Fermion Sector (Gauge-Ferm)**: Organic derivation of the Standard Model gauge group $\mathrm{SU}(3) \times \mathrm{SU}(2) \times \mathrm{U}(1)$ and chiral fermion representations.
8. **Dark Energy Mechanism (Dark-Energy)**: Non-anthropic derivation of the observed positive cosmological constant ($\rho_\Lambda \sim 10^{-47}\text{ GeV}^4$).
9. **Flavor Sector & Generations (Flavor-Gen)**: Explanation of exactly 3 generations and non-trivial mass hierarchies (e.g., Koide relation).
10. **Near-Term Falsifiability (Near-Test)**: Production of sharp, non-trivial predictions accessible to current or near-term instruments ($< 10\text{ years}$).
11. **Machine-Certified Formal Proofs (Formal-Proof)**: Complete verification of the core mathematical architecture in an interactive proof assistant (Lean 4, Coq, Isabelle).
12. **Community Maturity & Development (Comm-Mat)**: Decades of community cross-checks, literature volume, and institutional tenure.

## 3. Honest & Critical Examination of the Simplicial Framework's Limitations

Any theory aspiring to scientific credibility must subject its own vulnerabilities to ruthless peer-review scrutiny. Below is the detailed inventory of the **major challenges, bottlenecks, and risks** confronting Unified Simplicial Quantum Gravity:

- **The Reality**: String Theory has existed for over 50 years; LQG for over 35 years. Both possess thousands of active researchers, dedicated institutes (IAS, Perimeter, Max Planck), and specialized peer-reviewed journals.
- **The Challenge**: The Unified Simplicial Framework is in its foundational phase. Despite its 141 machine-certified proofs and Zenodo monograph releases, it lacks an international network of independent research groups actively running separate cross-checks. Overcoming scientific conservatism requires years of presentations at major international conferences (GR, Marcel Grossmann, Strings, Loops).

### B. The Combinatorial Simulation Bottleneck
- **The Reality**: The non-local Simplicial Beta-Laplacian $(-\Delta_{\Delta_4})^\alpha$ couples vertices across power-law distance kernels $\mathcal{K}_\alpha(x, y) \propto |x - y|^{-(d+2\alpha)}$.
- **The Challenge**: In a continuous local PDE, computing dynamics scales linearly $O(N)$ with grid size. For non-local simplicial operators, the dense interaction matrix scales as $O(N^2)$ to $O(N^3)$. Simulating a realistic cosmological volume containing billions of pentachora requires developing novel, highly optimized $\mathcal{H}$-matrix (hierarchical matrix) algorithms and GPU-accelerated sparse solvers.
