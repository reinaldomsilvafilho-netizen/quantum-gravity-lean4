\chapter*{Author's Preface}
This comprehensive monograph consolidates the multidisciplinary investigations developed across three fundamental programs of contemporary theoretical research: functional tensor algebra and geometric flows, fractional calculus on continuous Pascal simplices, and the variational theory of minimax extrinsic curvature with causal Jordan embeddings.

The objective of this work is to develop the mathematics of these three programs rigorously, and to state precisely what they do and do not imply for the problem of reconciling General Relativity with Quantum Field Theory. Throughout, statements labelled \emph{Theorem}, \emph{Proposition} or \emph{Lemma} are proved in the text or cited as classical results. Statements whose proof is not available are labelled \emph{Conjecture}. Phenomenological parametrizations and heuristic analogies are identified as such, and Chapter~13 records order-of-magnitude estimates of the observational signatures discussed in Part~IV.

\section*{Status of the Verification Material}
The accompanying repository contains two kinds of supporting material. Their status is stated here explicitly.
{\sloppy
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Numerical scripts.} The scripts \texttt{verify\_chapXX\_numerical.py} (one per chapter) and the auxiliary checking scripts of the repository evaluate formulas of the text numerically, for example by comparing closed forms with direct quadrature. Such checks catch algebraic and numerical errors. They do not replace proofs, and a check can only be as independent as its reference computation. The scripts are being revised so that every test compares against an independent computation.
    \item \textbf{\textsc{Lean 4} files} (\texttt{formal\_proofs\_book}, \texttt{formal\_proofs\_lean4}). The modules under \texttt{formal\_proofs\_book/Book} are a \emph{naming skeleton}: each chapter's results are recorded as named structures whose fields are Boolean or floating-point placeholders, and the accompanying Lean ``theorems'' only restate their hypotheses. They therefore verify \emph{no} mathematical content of this book and should not be read as a formalization. A genuine formalization against \textsc{Mathlib}, beginning with the elementary algebraic lemmas (e.g.\ the constraint bounds of Chapter~12, Theorem~3.1), is in progress in \texttt{formal\_proofs\_book/BookReal}. Only results listed there with their \textsc{Lean} statement should be regarded as machine-checked.
\end{enumerate}
\par}

{\sloppy
The source files, numerical scripts, and \textsc{Lean} material are hosted at
\begin{center}
\url{https://github.com/reinaldomsilvafilho-netizen/quantum-gravity-lean4}
\end{center}
together with an audit log (\texttt{WORKPLAN.md}) recording known issues, corrections, and the verification status of each result.
}
