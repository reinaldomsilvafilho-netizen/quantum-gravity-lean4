# Reconstruction of the Zenodo v2 source of Vol. I (2026-10-06)

The v2 `.tex` of *Beyond the Spectrum*, Vol. I (Zenodo version 22699282, 2026-09-11; the same text sits in the latest trilogy record 22866175) was lost. The local `paper_functional_realizations.tex` held the older v1 text (Zenodo 22644744).

## Procedure

1. Backup of the v1 `.tex`: `_arquivo/backup_tex_2026-10-06/Manuscritos_Avulsos/paper_functional_realizations/paper_functional_realizations.tex`. Logged in `_arquivo/backup_tex_2026-10-06/MANIFESTO.tsv`.
2. Text extraction with PyMuPDF from three files:
   - the v1 PDF (`audit/zenodo_v22644744_vol1.pdf`);
   - the standalone v2 PDF (`beyond_the_spectrum_files/volume_1_functional_realizations.pdf`, 17 pp.);
   - the Vol. I pages of the trilogy (`audit/zenodo_latest_vol1.pdf`).
3. Word-stream diff (`difflib`), v1 PDF against v2 PDF. The diff lists every change. Each change was then applied to the v1 `.tex` with the Edit tool.
4. Trilogy pages against the standalone v2 PDF: the only difference is the trilogy running head. Page numbers below refer to the standalone v2 PDF.
5. Check: the reconstructed `.tex` was compiled and its text diffed against the v2 PDF.
   - The result has 17 pages.
   - The only remaining differences are typographic: bracket sizes in three norms of eq. (5.19), and italic spacing in the extracted symbol "T" (the product manifold; v2 probably used a calligraphic or blackboard T, the reconstruction uses `\mathbb{T}`).
   - No word differs.
6. Copy of the pure v2 reconstruction, before any correction: `_arquivo/backup_tex_2026-10-06/Manuscritos_Avulsos/paper_functional_realizations/paper_functional_realizations_v2_reconstructed.tex`.
