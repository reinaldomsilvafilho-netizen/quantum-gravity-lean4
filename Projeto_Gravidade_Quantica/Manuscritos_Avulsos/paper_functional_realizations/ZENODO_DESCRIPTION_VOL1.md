**Volume I: Functional Realizations of Matrices and Tensors, Emerging Invariants, and Geometric Measures**

Volume I maps matrices and tensors to functions (forms on spheres, graphons, trigonometric polynomials, splines) and studies the quantities they carry. Dirichlet energy and total variation change under a relabelling of the indices, so the spectrum does not determine them; Morse indices and the graphon cut distance are permutation-invariant geometric readings.

**Proved, or classical with references:**
- **Dirichlet energy:** equals ‖DA‖²_F + ‖AD‖²_F. It can differ by a factor n² between permutation-conjugate matrices, though not for every matrix.
- **Total variation:** an exact formula for step realizations, with its coarea reading.
- **Morse indices:** for quadratic forms on spheres.
- **Cut norm:** equivalence with the L^∞→L¹ norm, and graphon compactness.
- **Rank one:** existence of best rank-one tensor approximations.
- **Critical scaling:** a two-sided bound showing that d^{−1/2} is the critical scaling for the spectral norm of Gaussian k-tensors.
- **Tail:** an exact Gaussian tail for random evaluations.

**Imported:** from Auffinger–Ben Arous–Černý, the critical-point complexity ½ log(d−1) of the isotropic spherical d-spin landscape.

**Proposals and conjectures:**
- a Sobolev training penalty;
- graphon width scaling;
- a link between replica symmetry breaking and sub-level-set connectivity.

**Limits:**
- the rank r ≥ 2 ill-posedness of De Silva–Lim is not removed;
- hypergraph compactness is only cited;
- nothing is formally verified.
