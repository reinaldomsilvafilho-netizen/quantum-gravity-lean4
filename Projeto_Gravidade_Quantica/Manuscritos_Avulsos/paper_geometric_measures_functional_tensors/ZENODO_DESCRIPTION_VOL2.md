**Beyond the Spectrum II: Metric Measure Geometry, Persistent Homology, and Non-Commutative Invariants of Functional Tensor Manifolds (v3)**

A functional realization turns a matrix into a function on a domain. This volume studies invariants of that function which, unlike eigenvalues, depend on where the entries sit.

Proved here:
- a sharp L¹ bound for the Wasserstein distance between matrices of different sizes, and a metric-speed bound for kernel realizations;
- explicit isospectral 3×3 and 5×5 pairs separated by step-realization persistence diagrams (persistent entropy separates only the 5×5 pair, and does not separate isospectral matrices in general);
- a c*εk³ law for the level-set Willmore energy of a trigonometric checkerboard;
- Besov index 1/p for step realizations;
- on the torus T², the graded antisymmetrized cyclic cocycle equals −4i·deg F (the ungraded one is not topological);
- the quantum Fisher information volume vanishes iff the derivatives of the density field are everywhere linearly dependent.

Classical results applied: Bakry–Émery, bottleneck stability, Cheeger, the wavefront set of a jump, Connes' trace theorem.
