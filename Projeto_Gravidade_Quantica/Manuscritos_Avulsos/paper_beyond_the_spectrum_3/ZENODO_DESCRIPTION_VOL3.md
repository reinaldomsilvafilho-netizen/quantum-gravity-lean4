**Beyond the Spectrum III: Higher Invariants, Bipartite Kernels, and Non-Equilibrium Geometry of Functional Realizations**, version 3 (corrected)

This volume studies functional realizations of matrices through invariants from symplectic topology, microlocal sheaf theory, nonlinear spectral theory, stochastic thermodynamics, bipartite kernels and geometric measure theory. Proved or cited-classical results include:

- Hessian bounds on the first Ekeland–Hofer capacity of convex sublevel sets;
- Whitney regularity of transverse rank stratifications;
- for weighted p-Laplacians, (λ_p)^{1/p} → 1/inradius as p → ∞, for any weight bounded above and below;
- W₂ contraction for κ-convex Langevin potentials, and the Jarzynski identity e^{−ΔF} = Tr B / Tr A;
- trace and rank bounds for partial traces of bipartite kernels;
- the simplicial Mellin transform is entire;
- a reach bound for compact level sets, and the tube formula below the reach.

Counterexamples show that three stronger statements fail. The holographic relation S_R = 2E_W is recalled as a physical proposal only.

Version 3 corrects false version-2 statements (Cheeger limit, Euler-characteristic formula, thermodynamic-length bound, finite rank, Mellin poles) and withdraws the formal-verification claim; the earlier Lean files are a placeholder skeleton. Corrections are AI-assisted and AI-checked, not checked by an independent human expert.
