\begin{theorem}[Minimax Regularization of the Hamiltonian Constraint]
\label{thm:minimax_hamiltonian_regularization}

\begin{proof}

\end{equation}
Substituting this bound into the Hamiltonian constraint \eqref{eq:wdw_hamiltonian} gives:
\begin{equation}
